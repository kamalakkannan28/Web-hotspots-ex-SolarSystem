🌌 Solar System Explorer 🚀

The Solar System consists of the Sun and all the celestial bodies that revolve around it. The Sun is a huge star located at the center of the Solar System. Eight planets orbit the Sun due to its strong gravitational force. These planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. The Solar System also includes moons, asteroids, comets, and meteoroids. Each planet has its own unique size, structure, and atmosphere. The Solar System formed billions of years ago from a cloud of gas and dust. It is part of our galaxy called the Milky Way.

🌑 Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet in the Solar System. It is closest to the Sun. It has no atmosphere to trap heat. Temperatures vary greatly. Mercury orbits the Sun very quickly.

  • 📏 Diameter: 4,879 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 88 Earth days
  • 🌕 Moons: None

🌕 Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun. It is similar in size to Earth. It has a thick carbon dioxide atmosphere. Heat is trapped strongly. Venus is the hottest planet.

  • 📏 Diameter: 12,104 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 225 Earth days
  • 🌕 Moons: None

🌍 Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun. It supports life. Air and water are present. The atmosphere protects life. One orbit takes one year.

  • 📏 Diameter: 12,742 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 365 days
  • 🌕 Moons: 1

🔴 Mars

Mars is called the Red Planet. Iron oxide gives red color. It has mountains and valleys. Scientists study Mars. It has two moons.

  • 📏 Diameter: 6,779 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 687 Earth days
  • 🌕 Moons: 2

🟠 Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet. It is a gas giant. It has many moons. A strong magnetic field exists. The Great Red Spot is a storm.

  • 📏 Diameter: 139,820 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 12 Earth years
  • 🌕 Moons: 79+

💍 Saturn

Saturn has beautiful rings. It is a gas giant. Rings are ice and rock. Many moons orbit it. It is very large.

  • 📏 Diameter: 116,460 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 29 Earth years
  • 🌕 Moons: 80+

❄️ Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant. It rotates on its side. Seasons are extreme. It looks blue-green. Methane causes the color.

  • 📏 Diameter: 50,724 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 84 Earth years
  • 🌕 Moons: 27

🌊 Neptune

Neptune is farthest from the Sun. It is an ice giant. Winds are extremely strong. It looks deep blue. The atmosphere is cold and stormy.

  • 📏 Diameter: 49,244 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 165 Earth years
  • 🌕 Moons: 14
