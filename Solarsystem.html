🌌 Solar System Explorer 🚀

The Solar System consists of the Sun and all the celestial bodies that revolve around it. The Sun is the center of the Solar System and provides heat and light. Eight planets move around the Sun due to gravitational force. These planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. The Solar System also includes moons, asteroids, comets, and meteoroids. Each planet has unique features and characteristics. Earth is the only known planet that supports life. Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System. The Solar System formed billions of years ago. It is part of the Milky Way galaxy.

🌑 Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet in the Solar System. It is the closest planet to the Sun. Mercury has no atmosphere to trap heat. Because of this, it experiences extreme temperatures. It completes one orbit around the Sun very quickly.

  • 📏 Diameter: 4,879 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 88 Earth days
  • 🌕 Moons: None

🌕 Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun. It is similar in size to Earth. Venus has a thick carbon dioxide atmosphere. This causes a strong greenhouse effect. It is the hottest planet in the Solar System.

  • 📏 Diameter: 12,104 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 225 Earth days
  • 🌕 Moons: None

🌍 Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun. It is the only planet known to support life. Earth has air, water, and suitable temperature. Its atmosphere protects living beings. Earth completes one orbit in one year.

  • 📏 Diameter: 12,742 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 365 days
  • 🌕 Moons: 1

🔴 Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet. Its red color is due to iron oxide. Mars has mountains and valleys. Scientists search for signs of water on Mars. It has two small moons.

  • 📏 Diameter: 6,779 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 687 Earth days
  • 🌕 Moons: 2

🟠 Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System. It is a gas giant made mainly of hydrogen. Jupiter has a strong magnetic field. It has many moons. The Great Red Spot is a giant storm.

  • 📏 Diameter: 139,820 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 12 Earth years
  • 🌕 Moons: 79+

💍 Saturn

Saturn is famous for its beautiful rings. It is a gas giant planet. The rings are made of ice and rock. Saturn has many moons. It is the second-largest planet.

  • 📏 Diameter: 116,460 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 29 Earth years
  • 🌕 Moons: 80+

❄️ Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant planet. It rotates on its side. This causes extreme seasons. Uranus appears blue-green in color. Methane gas causes its color.

  • 📏 Diameter: 50,724 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 84 Earth years
  • 🌕 Moons: 27

🌊 Neptune

Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun. It is an ice giant planet. Neptune has very strong winds. It appears deep blue in color. It has a cold and stormy atmosphere.

  • 📏 Diameter: 49,244 km
  • 🕒 Orbit: 165 Earth years
  • 🌕 Moons: 14
